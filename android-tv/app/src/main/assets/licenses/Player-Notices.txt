The built-in player uses AndroidX Media3 1.11.1 (Apache License 2.0) and FFmpeg 6.0.1 (LGPL 2.1 or later).

Just Player (moneytoo/Player) is released under the Unlicense. Its subtitle MIME mapping, renderer-priority approach and same-resolution display-mode matching were used as references and adapted for TorrFrame. Its UI, application and modified ExoPlayer binaries are not included.
Reference commit: c593ac3f5ca0784d06f424984abf8650f5bd1f8b
https://github.com/moneytoo/Player

The Media3 FFmpeg audio renderer and JNI bridge are from AndroidX Media3 tag 1.11.1, commit 8c6678b657ede1e7883fc164ef73ed483c7796c3. Original copyright and Apache license headers are preserved.
https://github.com/androidx/media/tree/1.11.1/libraries/decoder_ffmpeg

FFmpeg is built with GPL, nonfree and version3 components disabled. Only internal audio decoders, avutil and swresample are enabled. No external codec libraries are linked.
Copyright (c) FFmpeg contributors. See FFmpeg-LGPL-2.1.txt.
https://ffmpeg.org

Corresponding source, configuration and relinking instructions are distributed with the project in third-party/ffmpeg and android-tv/player-ffmpeg. The scripts/build-player-ffmpeg.sh script rebuilds the replaceable libffmpegJNI.so for both ARM ABIs. Source archive SHA-256:
9b16b8731d78e596b4be0d720428ca42df642bb2d78342881ff7f5bc29fc9623
Source release: https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-6.0.1.tar.xz

You may modify and rebuild these libraries, relink/repackage them with the application, and reverse engineer the application for debugging such modifications as permitted by the LGPL. No separate player artwork or UI assets from Just Player are used.
